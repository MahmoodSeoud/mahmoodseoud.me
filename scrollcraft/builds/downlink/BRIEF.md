# BRIEF: downlink (mahmoodseoud.me)

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Mahmood was offered the
eight-question interview and answered "Your call, author it". Everything below
is an authored decision unless marked as evidence. Evidence comes from the
existing `index.html` and `assets/` in this repository.

## What this is, who it is for

Evidence: a personal site for a computer science student who writes embedded C
for a CubeSat camera controller (DISCOSAT), builds AI tooling at PeopleNet, and
has one published paper (COPA 2024).
Authored: the audience is people who hire or supervise engineers in space
technology and applied AI.

- **Belief to install:** he ships software that has to work where nobody can
  reach it to fix it.
- **One action, one label:** `Email Mahmood`.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Quiet, precise, cold, observational, a little wonder. References:
   a museum object label; the raw frames in a spacecraft imaging press kit; a
   contact sheet with grease-pencil marks.
2. **Journey.** A satellite over Denmark. Then the same picture as the sensor
   really records it, developing under the scroll. Then the other things he has
   built, as a collection with plain labels. Then the record of where he
   studied and worked. Then the person, and how to write to him.
3. **Energy.** Calm open. One intense passage (the develop). A relaxed lateral
   walk. A compressed, quiet ledger. A still close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve and the peak below.
5. **The one thing no other site does.** The page runs a real Bayer
   demosaic in the browser, on the hero picture, driven by scroll.
6. **Range.** Dense and editorial in the labels, premium-minimal in the
   grounds. Dark, cold, one warm accent taken from the sunlit limb in the render.
7. **World or scenes.** Distinct scenes. A collection is separate objects.
8. **Assets.** Evidence: a 4000x3000 DISCO-2 orbit render, a portrait, a LEGO
   before/after pair, two SysMentor demo GIFs, a paper preview, logos. No
   generation key is configured, so nothing is generated. Every picture on the
   page is his own.

## Decisions made after the brief

- **Grammar:** Gallery / catalog. The visitor's real question about a portfolio
  is "what has he built". Filmic one-shot lost because the work is separate
  objects, not one argument. Chaptered editorial forbids media above the fold,
  and the orbit render is the best asset here. Live surface would need a real
  product to operate. Continuous world needs a flight that was never rendered.
  Typographic poster wastes the imagery. Split stage has no two-sided argument.
  Rhythmic cutlist is the wrong pulse for a quiet engineer's record.
- **Fingerprint gate:** the registry was empty, so there was nothing to clear.
- **Score:** hero (layered parallax, flow) > develop (pin, bespoke canvas, peak,
  span 4.4) > scratches (flow + reveal) > collection (pan, tilt, one real
  counter) > record (flow) > maker (pin, 1.35).
